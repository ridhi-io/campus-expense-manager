# Project Statement

## Problem Statement
College students make frequent payments for food, travel, education, shopping and entertainment. Without consistent records it is difficult to calculate monthly spending, identify high-spending categories, or check whether a budget is being exceeded.

## Scope
Campus Expense Manager is an offline command-line system for recording, viewing, searching, updating and deleting expenses; setting monthly budgets; calculating monthly/category-wise spending; and storing data locally in CSV files. It does not process real bank transactions or payments.

## Target Users
- College students
- Students living away from home
- Students wanting a simple offline budget tracker

## High-Level Features
1. Expense CRUD
2. Category-based organization
3. Search
4. Monthly budget configuration
5. Budget utilization
6. Category-wise analytics
7. CSV persistence
8. Input validation
9. Error handling
10. Logging
